Questions

Simple answers.

Can I keep my number?

That is the goal. Norm is designed around a normal phone-plan switch, including number transfer.

Is this active today?

Not yet. This is a prototype to test the offer, pricing, and early interest before launch.

Will it support eSIM?

Yes, the intended first version is eSIM-first so activation can be simple and digital.

What does ownership mean?

It means your monthly participation should count toward something more durable than a bill receipt. The exact reward structure is still being designed.
