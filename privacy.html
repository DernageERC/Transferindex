Privacy Policy

Norm is currently an early access prototype. We collect only the information you submit through the waitlist form.

What we collect

Email address, current monthly phone bill if provided, signup source, page URL, and signup timestamp.

How we use it

We use this information to understand early interest, contact waitlist members, and improve the Norm phone plan concept.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not process payments through this prototype.

Contact

For questions, contact Norm through the same channel where you received the waitlist link.
